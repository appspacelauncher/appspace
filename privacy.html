Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 16, 2025

Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to all applications developed and published by us ("we," "our," or "us"), including but not limited to macOS applications. This policy explains how we handle information when you use any of our apps.

Data Collection and Storage

What We Don't Collect

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information
  • No Usage Analytics: We do not track how you use our apps
  • No Advertising Data: We do not collect data for advertising or marketing purposes
  • No Cloud Sync: We do not upload or sync your data to our servers

What Is Stored Locally

Any data created or used by our apps is stored locally on your device and never leaves it unless you explicitly choose otherwise:

  • App Preferences: Settings and configuration data
  • User Content: Any content, files, or references you create or manage within the app
  • Security Information: Optional passwords or security settings stored securely using operating-system-provided mechanisms (such as the macOS Keychain)

Third-Party Services

Platform Services

  • App Stores: Used for distribution, payments, and updates
  • In-App Purchases / Subscriptions: Managed entirely by the platform provider (e.g., Apple StoreKit)
  • Operating System Security: Secure storage features provided by the OS

We do not integrate third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or external data collection services.

Network Access

Our apps may access the network only when required for core functionality, such as:

  • Platform purchase or subscription verification
  • Accessing external resources or locations that you explicitly choose

We do not operate our own servers for storing user data.

Data Security

  • All data remains on your device
  • We follow platform-recommended security best practices
  • Sensitive information is stored using secure, system-provided encryption

Your Rights

Because we do not collect or process personal data, there is no personal information for us to access, modify, or delete on your behalf. You retain full control over your data on your device.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. As no personal data is collected, this policy applies equally to users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date and publishing the revised policy.

Contact Us

  • Email: apps@iparrow.com

Legal Compliance

This Privacy Policy is intended to comply with applicable privacy laws, including:

  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
  • Other applicable regional privacy regulations

All of our apps are designed with privacy in mind. Your data stays on your device.
